    SYNTAX

    Chords

    [G]Chord placed inline, right before the lyric it lands on — e.g. [G]Amazing [C]grace
    A line that's mostly bare chords with no brackets (e.g. A B C#) is also detected automatically and paired with the lyric line below it, rendered in monospace to keep the columns aligned as written.

    Song metadata

    Key: GPlain "Label: value" line near the top
    {key: G}Curly-brace directive, same idea
    Also recognized: Capo, Tempo/BPM, Time, Tuning
    Scroll: 1.2Sets this song's autoscroll speed (0.2–10). Just presets the slider — scroll always starts paused, you still press ▶ Scroll yourself

    Sections

    ChorusA line with just a section name (Verse, Chorus, Bridge, Intro, Outro, Pre-Chorus, Interlude, Tag, Hook, Refrain, Instrumental, Solo, Ending, Break, Vamp, Turnaround), optionally numbered or with a colon
    [Verse 1]Same, in brackets

    Comments

    # noteShown italic and muted, not sung

    Line formatting (prefix, applies to the whole line)

    *textBold
    /textItalic
    !textBold italic
    _textUnderline
    &red:textColored text
    >yellow:textHighlighted text
    Prefixes can be stacked, e.g. *&red:text for bold red

    Inline formatting (wraps just a word/phrase)

    <*word>Bold
    </word>Italic
    <!word>Bold italic
    <_word>Underline
    <&red:word>Colored word
    <>yellow:word>Highlighted word

    Colors

    black, white, red, orange, yellow, green, blue, lightblue, purple, pink, grey — or a hex code like &#ff6600:
